## Corporate-action notes

- **ETERNAL**: Renamed from Zomato Ltd in 2024; same listed entity (IPO 2021-07-23). Financial history is continuous across the rename -- no corporate-action discontinuity.
- **HDFCBANK**: HDFC Ltd merged into HDFC Bank effective 2023-07-01. Total equity roughly 2.4x'd and shares outstanding rose ~36% between FY23 and FY24 as a result. The period ending 2023-03-31 is the pre-merger, standalone bank and is not comparable to FY24 onward -- discovered via a Screener.in spot-check gap on ROE/ROCE (Phase 2 review).
- **JIOFIN**: Jio Financial Services listed on 2023-08-21 after its demerger from Reliance Industries. The period ending 2023-03-31 reflects a pre-listing shell entity (Reliance Strategic Investments) with minimal operations, not comparable to JFS's operating business.
- **TMPV**: Yahoo's TMPV.NS carries the pre-demerger Tata Motors Limited's trading history (listed since 1991). Periods ending before 2025-04-01 include the commercial-vehicle business and are not comparable to standalone Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles.
